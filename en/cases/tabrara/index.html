Tabrara iOS

The Tabrara seller app was only on Android. I brought it to iPhone and followed the iOS guidelines

I worked alone, from requirements to handoff

Task

Bring the seller app to iPhone with the same features as on Android

Solution

iOS navigation, components and gestures, Sign in with Apple, App Store subscription and account deletion

Result

Why iPhone

In Tabrara, sellers run an online shop from their phone: catalog, orders, reports and team. The app was only on Android, so sellers with an iPhone could not use it. The service grows when sellers recommend it to each other, and every seller with an iPhone dropped out of this chain

Stays as on Android

  • All seller features
  • Colors, illustrations and screen content
  • Data and server

Changes for iOS

  • HIG navigation and gestures
  • System components in the Liquid Glass style
  • SF Symbols icons and the SF Pro font

Required to pass App Review

  1. 1Sign in with Apple
  2. 2Subscription through Apple in‑app purchase
  3. 3Account deletion in the app

Out of scope: the customer storefront on the web, iPad and new features from the growth strategy

What I studied

Before the screens, I studied the Android app, other seller apps and Apple’s requirements

  • Android mockups

    All five sections of the latest version. Where there were several options, for example the product list and onboarding, I chose one and wrote down the reason

  • 8 seller apps

    Ecwid, Shopify, Ozon Seller, Etsy Seller, Square and others. None of them has an action button in the order row. Sellers change the status on the order screen

  • Apple and iOS 26

    How Liquid Glass changes navigation and which App Review rules affect the screens: sign-in, subscription and account deletion

Key finding

Phone and address on the order card

None of the 8 apps shows them in the order list. In a poll of Tabrara sellers, phone, name and address were chosen most often. So on the iOS card they come first, and the order number and time are less visible

Orders, products, reports and shop

Orders come first because sellers work with them every day. In the order and product lists, the tab bar gets smaller when you scroll

Decision 2

How an Android seller finds their shop on iPhone

On Android, sellers signed in with a phone number. On iPhone, the first button is “Sign in with Apple”. Without linked sign-in methods, a seller would get an empty account and think the orders were lost

    Apple, Google, phone or email

  • , open large

    Find your shop

  • , open large

    Email match

    The app finds the shop and asks for a code from an email

  • , open large

    One account

    All sign-in methods in one list. You can add more

Options I considered

  • Backup path

    Hint on the name screen

    “Already selling on Tabrara? Sign in to another account”. I kept it, but one line is not enough. It is easy to miss

  • Rejected

    Merge by email without a code

    If the Apple ID email matches, open the old shop at once. Without a code, someone could get into another person’s shop

  • Chosen

    The “Have a Shop?” screen

    Every new account is first asked about a shop. The seller finds it by phone or email and links it after entering a code

Decision 3

Weight to the gram

A seller wrote to support that two decimal places were not enough. With expensive products, every small underweight costs money

  • , open large

    Minimum and step

    The seller sets the minimum order and the step for customers

  • , open large

    Three decimals

    Weight is entered to the gram. The stepper is wider

  • , open large

    Editing items

    The exact weight after weighing. If the order is paid, the app warns about the new total

  • The total is based on the exact weight

    €12 × 0.485 kg

    €5.82

Options I considered

  • Rejected

    Grams below 1 kg

    Grams below 1 kg and kilograms above. Two units in one field make a 1,000× mistake easy

  • Rejected

    Separate weight field

    The exact weight goes in a field next to the stepper. The row of a product sold by weight becomes taller and more complex than other rows

  • Chosen

    Wide stepper

    The same control as for products sold by the piece, only wider, with three decimal places. The downside: the price in the row wraps to two lines

Decision 4

Tabrara PRO on iPhone

I could not move the subscription screens from Android as they were. The purchase goes through Apple, and Apple has its own rules for terms, restoring purchases and account deletion

  • , open large

    Paywall

    Price, renewal terms and “Restore Purchases”

  • , open large

    PRO from Android

    A subscription bought on Android also works on iPhone. There is no need to buy it again

  • , open large

    Account deletion

    Right in the app. It does not cancel the Apple subscription, and a banner warns about this

  • , open large

    Notifications

    The app asks for permission after the seller first shares the shop link, when the benefit is clear

App Store Review Guidelines: 3.1.1 (in‑app purchase), 3.1.2 (subscriptions) and 5.1.1(v) (account deletion)
