Shifted

UX/⁠UI case study · Android

AI-⁠powered used car marketplace

An online platform for buying and selling cars where every car and seller is automatically checked for honesty

Role
Researcher and designer
Platform
Android
Research
20 interviews, 3 competitors

01 · Goal and role

Is there a user segment that needs this kind of service

Business goal

Assess the market potential of a service for buying and selling used cars and determine whether there is a user segment whose need for such a service is strong enough to launch an MVP

My role

  • Designed and ran qualitative research: 20 in-⁠depth interviews with used car buyers to uncover their buying scenarios and key barriers
  • Analyzed 3 competitors (Drom, Auto.ru, Avito) to understand their value proposition, user scenarios and unmet audience needs
  • Built a CJM from the insights, identified problems and their solutions, formulated product hypotheses and proposed an MVP focused on simplifying seller and car verification
  • Designed the interface of the mobile app with the AI assistant

02 · Problems

Buying a used car is one big problem, and searching is the hardest part

  • 16 of 20

    respondents fear buying a low-quality car through online listings

  • 6 of 20

    wasted a lot of time driving to see a car in much worse condition than expected

  • 1.5 hours

    is the average drive to a car from a listing, only to be disappointed. And the same way back

  • 01

    No trust in listings

    People are afraid of being cheated: the seller hides the car’s flaws, sells it, and then it is unclear what to do

  • 02

    Lots of resellers

    They inflate the price for resale without improving the car’s condition and may hide its real history

  • 03

    Search takes a lot of time

    You have to drive to see every listing you like. Sometimes far away. And the car may turn out to be bad

  • 04

    Paid reports

    They cost around €2 each and still cannot guarantee the quality of the car

05 · Solution

A mobile app with the AI assistant

The insights led to a product solution: a mobile app with an AI assistant

  • 01

    Check

    Checks cars for hidden defects and sellers for suspicious activity

  • 02

    Search

    Helps find a car by turning a text query into filters. Write a request in free form and get listings

  • 03

    Inspection

    Helps check a car in person, replacing a professional car picker. Send photos to the chat and get an analysis of the car

12 · Hypotheses and MVP

Five hypotheses and the MVP scope

The hypotheses are based on the research insights and tied to product features

  1. 01

    If the card shows the AI check status and a risk tag, buyers filter out doubtful listings before a trip and fear being cheated less

    No trust in listings

  2. 02

    If risks are visible before an inspection, the number of useless trips to bad cars goes down

    Search takes a lot of time

  3. 03

    If the check is built into the platform, buyers do not need outside reports for €2 that do not guarantee quality

    Paid reports

  4. 04

    If there is text query search next to the filters, novices find suitable cars faster, while experienced drivers keep their familiar path

    Two audience groups

  5. 05

    If the AI assistant analyzes photos of a car and gives a step-by-⁠step guide, buyers can inspect a car without a professional car picker

    Hidden defects

In the MVP

  • Search by filters and by text query with refining of results
  • A card with the AI check status and a risk tag
  • A listing with specifications and features
  • The AI assistant in chat: explaining risks and checking by photos

Not included, planned for later

  • Payment and the deal inside the platform
  • Inspection with a live expert
  • Extended tools for sellers

13 · Conclusion

A segment with a real need exists

The research showed that used car buyers have a strong need to verify listings: 16 of 20 respondents fear buying a low-quality car, and the existing competitor paths do not solve the problem. The project combines prompt-based car search with AI analysis of listings. The solution is meant to simplify search and make buying more transparent
